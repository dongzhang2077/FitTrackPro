Project Third Submission

For the purpose of your third and last submission for the course project please submit the following:

1) The zipped version of your Android Studio project that contains your completed work.

Please keep in mind that in order to give you credit for your work, I need to test the app, so please include anything you believe I need to test your app.
It is necessary to mention the version of Android Studio used to develop the app and the names of the emulators and devices and APIs used in testing the app at this point.
For the rest of the document, please mention any features which have been completed or developed since your second submission.
